💊 MedTracker

Medication reminder & pill tracker

MedTracker helps you take the right medicine at the right time. Log each dose with one tap, see exactly when the next one is due, and know when you are running low — all stored privately on your device.

Features

  • Flexible schedules

    Every X hours, fixed times of day, or as needed with a minimum gap between doses.

  • Pill counting & refills

    Track how many pills are left, how many days your supply lasts, and get low-stock warnings.

  • Reminders

    Browser notifications plus recurring calendar reminders for Google, Apple and Outlook Calendar.

  • Adherence history

    See every dose you took or skipped and your adherence over the last 7 or 30 days.

  • Private & offline

    No account and no server. Your data never leaves your device and the app works offline.

  • Seven languages

    English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish and French.

  • Doctor visit planner

    Follow-up reminders, a notebook for symptoms and questions, and a visit summary to show your doctor.

Frequently asked questions

Is MedTracker free?
Yes. MedTracker is completely free, with no ads, no subscription and no sign-up.
Where is my data stored?
Only in your browser on this device. You can download a backup file at any time and restore it on another device.
Will I get reminders when the app is closed?
Browser notifications work while the app is open or installed. For guaranteed reminders, export your schedule to your phone calendar.
Can I track a course of antibiotics?
Yes. Enter the total number of pills and MedTracker shows your progress and how many days are left in the course.
Does MedTracker give medical advice?
No. It is a personal reminder tool. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.
Can it remind me about follow-up appointments?
Yes. Add the appointment, choose when to be reminded, and keep a list of things to tell your doctor. Before the visit, MedTracker builds a summary with your notes and medication history.

MedTracker is a personal reminder tool and does not provide medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.
